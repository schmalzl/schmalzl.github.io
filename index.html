ABOUT
SUBSONIC
An underground music player built for lifters.
A manually curated music collection focused on high-energy training music. No algorithms, no recommendations, no distractions.
I am a lifter myself and found myself switching to TikTok sounds again and again.
Soundcloud ads suck too. So I made an app that gets rid of ads and contains the essence of bass boosted songs.
If you have song recommendations, DM me on instagram (must be a registered user).
